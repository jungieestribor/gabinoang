My Story, Values & Personal Side

How a Passion for Technology Turned into a Calling for Teaching

My journey began when I assembled my first computer hardware and solved my first complex troubleshooting problem. For the first time, technology stopped being an intimidating black box and became an interactive canvas for creative problem-solving. That single spark ignited my lifelong passion for Information and Communications Technology (ICT) and digital literacy.

With a strong foundation in Computer Education and Technical Support, I realized that true digital fluency isn't about memorizing software menus—it's about hands-on creation and practical skill-building. Over the years as an ICT educator, I’ve dedicated my career to demystifying technology, guiding students through hardware, software, and digital media, and empowering them to navigate today's tech-driven world with confidence.
